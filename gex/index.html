Per-strike data

Data: CBOE delayed quotes (~15 min lag, free, no key) by default, or Tradier when the server is started with a TRADIER_TOKEN. Convention: dealers long calls / short puts (the classic naive GEX assumption) — same as most public GEX tools. Vanna & charm are computed with Black-Scholes from each option's quoted IV. The "VIX proxy" follows the CBOE VIX methodology (OTM mid quotes, 30-day interpolation) but from delayed retail data — call it VIX-style, not VIX. The convexity read is a labeled heuristic on the price of convexity (vol premium, term slope, wing richness), not observed order flow. Educational use only; not financial advice.
